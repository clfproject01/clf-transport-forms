Fuel Form — Driver Manual

How to submit your daily fuel receipt on your phone. This guide uses the exact words you will see on the screen.

Two branches now · Dua cawangan sekarang: there are now two branches — MTK (Mentakab) and KTN (Kuantan). Use YOUR branch's form link; it shows only your branch's lorries and drivers · Guna pautan borang cawangan ANDA sahaja; borang itu hanya menunjukkan lori dan pemandu cawangan anda. If you are unsure which link to use, ask your supervisor · Jika tidak pasti pautan mana, tanya penyelia anda.

What changed (please read): You now type in the Fuel Amount (RM) yourself, and it is required. Litres is now optional. You must still enter the Current Mileage (KM) (odometer reading) every single time you fuel up, and you must still take a photo of the receipt.

Before you start

  • You need your phone, your Staff ID (or your name), and the fuel receipt in your hand.
  • Submit the form right after you pump fuel, while you still have the receipt.
  • The notice at the top of the form says: "Important · Penting: Fill all fields. Late = lower score." Fill everything and submit on time.

Step-by-step

1. Open the form

Open the fuel form link on your phone (the page titled "FUEL — Daily Fuel Receipt · Resit Bahan Api"). The Fuel Date · Tarikh Pengisian Bahan Api is already filled in with today's date for you. Leave it as today unless the fuel was on a different day.

2. Choose your name (Driver)

Find the box labelled "Driver · Pemandu". Tap it and type your Staff ID (for example 448) or your name. A list will drop down — tap your name in the list. A yellow badge then shows your name to confirm it is selected.

The phone remembers you. Next time you may see a green "✓ Remembered" tag with your name already filled in — just check it is correct.

3. Second Driver (only if two of you share the lorry)

The next box is "Second Driver · Pemandu Kedua", marked "If two drivers share this lorry · optional". If you drive alone, skip this box. If another driver shares this lorry, type their Staff ID or name and tap them from the list.

The Second Driver must be a different person from you. If you pick the same name twice, the form will warn you: "Driver and Second Driver cannot be the same person."

4. Select your lorry (Vehicle Number)

Tap the box labelled "Vehicle Number · Nombor Kenderaan". It opens a list of your branch's lorry plate numbers. Tap the plate number of the lorry you fuelled.

5. Enter Current Mileage (KM) — every time

In the box labelled "Current Mileage (KM) · Bacaan Odometer Semasa" (it says "Enter current odometer reading · Masukkan bacaan odometer semasa"), type the number you see on the lorry's odometer right now.

  • This is the real, current odometer reading on the dashboard — not a guess and not last week's number.
  • You must enter it every time you fuel up.
  • Example shown in the box: e.g. 85420.

Why every time: the office uses today's reading minus your last reading to work out the distance you drove and your fuel-per-km. If the number is wrong or skipped, those figures are wrong.

6. Enter Fuel Amount (RM) — required

In the box labelled "Fuel Amount (RM) · Jumlah Bahan Api (RM)" (it says "Total RM on the receipt · Jumlah RM pada resit"), type the total ringgit amount printed on your receipt. For example e.g. 180.00.

  • Copy the number exactly from the receipt.
  • This box cannot be left empty — it is required now.

7. Enter Litres — optional

The box labelled "Litres · Liter" is marked "Optional · Pilihan". If the litres are on your receipt, type them (for example e.g. 120.5). If you are not sure, you may leave this box empty — it will not block you.

8. Take a photo of the receipt

Under "Receipt Photo · Foto Resit", tap the box that says "Upload Receipt Image · Muat Naik Gambar Resit" / "Tap to take photo · Ketik untuk ambil gambar". Your camera opens — take a clear photo of the whole receipt. When done, it shows "Photo taken! · Gambar diambil!" and a preview of the picture.

  • Make sure the RM amount and the litres are readable in the photo.
  • To redo it, tap the box again ("Tap to retake · Ketik untuk ambil semula").

The form reminds you: "Snap the receipt too · Ambil foto resit juga: we read it to double-check your amount." The system reads your photo and compares it to the RM you typed, so the photo must be clear.

9. Tick the confirm box and submit

Tick the checkbox that says "I confirm · Saya sahkan all info is accurate and matches the actual receipt. False submissions lower your score."

Then tap the dark button at the bottom: "Submit Fuel Record · Hantar Rekod Bahan Api".

You will see "Submitting... · Menghantar...", then a green tick and "Submitted! · Berjaya!" with your name, vehicle, and the time. You are done. To send another, tap "Submit Another · Hantar Lagi".

After submitting, the success screen asks you to check that your photo shows up in the supervisor dashboard within 1 minute. If something looks wrong, tell your supervisor.

If you did NOT refuel today

On days you do not pump any fuel, you do not fill the whole form. Instead, use the yellow box near the top:

  1. Choose your name in "Driver · Pemandu" (Step 2).
  2. Choose your lorry in "Vehicle Number · Nombor Kenderaan" (Step 4).
  3. Tap the yellow button "⛽ Declare No Fuel Today · Lapor Tiada Bahan Api Hari Ini".
  4. A check appears asking "Confirm: [your name] did NOT refuel today?" — tap OK.

That box tells you: "Tap below to declare 'No Fuel Today' — only Driver + Vehicle needed." You do not need mileage, amount, litres, or a photo for this.

Why this matters

Two numbers do the heavy lifting: the odometer reading (Current Mileage) and the Fuel Amount (RM). Together they let the boss see how much fuel costs per lorry and per kilometre, and spot problems early (too much fuel for the distance, missing days, or wrong readings). The receipt photo is the cross-check — the system reads it to confirm the RM you typed is correct. Accurate, honest entries protect your score; false ones lower it.

Common situations

Situation What to do
Two drivers share one lorry The driver who pumped the fuel fills the form. Put yourself in "Driver · Pemandu" and the other driver in "Second Driver · Pemandu Kedua". They must be two different people. Submit once — do not both submit the same receipt.
No fuel today Do not fill the full form. Pick your name and lorry, then tap "⛽ Declare No Fuel Today · Lapor Tiada Bahan Api Hari Ini" and confirm. (See the section above.)
You don't know the litres Leave "Litres · Liter" empty — it is optional. But you must still enter the Fuel Amount (RM) and take the photo.
Receipt photo is blurry Tap the photo box again to retake it. The system reads the photo to check your amount, so it must be clear.
The form won't submit It will point to whatever is missing (date, driver, vehicle, mileage, amount, photo, or the confirm tick). Fill the highlighted item, then tap "Submit Fuel Record · Hantar Rekod Bahan Api" again.
