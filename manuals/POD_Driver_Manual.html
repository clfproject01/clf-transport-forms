POD Form — Driver Manual

How to submit your Proof of Delivery on your phone, step by step. The on-screen text is shown in both English and Malay (for example Driver · Pemandu). This guide quotes the buttons and labels exactly as you see them.

Two branches now · Dua cawangan sekarang: there are now two branches — MTK (Mentakab) and KTN (Kuantan). Use YOUR branch's form link; it shows only your branch's lorries and drivers · Guna pautan borang cawangan ANDA sahaja; borang itu hanya menunjukkan lori dan pemandu cawangan anda. If you are unsure which link to use, ask your supervisor · Jika tidak pasti pautan mana, tanya penyelia anda.

The one rule: the form's top notice says — Important · Penting: Upload the SIGNED invoice on the same delivery day. Late or missing = penalty. Submit at each stop, not at the end of the day.

1. Open the form

  1. Open the POD link on your phone (the page is titled CLF Trading — POD Submission).
  2. At the top you will see POD and Proof of Delivery · Bukti Penghantaran. That confirms you are in the right form.
  3. Read the orange notice at the top once. It reminds you to upload the SIGNED invoice the same day.
  4. Delivery Date · Tarikh Penghantaran is filled in for you with today's date. Leave it as is unless told otherwise. (You cannot pick a future date.)

2. Enter your Staff ID (the Driver picker)

Under Driver · Pemandu there is a search box. The grey hint inside it reads Staff ID (e.g. 448) atau nama.

  1. Tap the box.
  2. Type your Staff ID number (example: 448) or your name.
  3. A list drops down. Each row shows the Staff ID in bold, then the name.
  4. Tap your row. The box now shows your ID and name together, and a tag ✓ [your name] appears under it.

The next time you open the form on the same phone, your name may already be filled with a green tag ✓ Remembered. If it is correct, leave it. If it is the wrong person, just type and pick the right one.

If you ever see the hint change to ⚠ Failed to load — refresh the page, close and reopen the link (you need a connection so the staff list can load).

Second Driver (only if two of you share the lorry)

The next box is Second Driver · Pemandu Kedua, marked If two drivers · optional. Its hint reads Staff ID atau nama (optional).

  • If you drive alone: skip this box. Leave it empty.
  • If two drivers: tap it, type the second person's Staff ID or name, and pick from the list (same as above).
  • You cannot put the same person in both boxes. If you do, you will see: Driver and Second Driver cannot be the same person.

3. Select the lorry / vehicle number

  1. Tap Vehicle Number · Nombor Kenderaan.
  2. It opens a list that starts with — Select vehicle · Pilih kenderaan —.
  3. Pick your plate number from your branch's lorry list.
  4. Choose carefully — this number decides which delivery list loads next.

4. The form loads today's deliveries for that lorry

As soon as the date and vehicle are both set, the form checks for today's job. You will briefly see Checking delivery list… · Menyemak….

  • If a shipment is found: a list appears under the heading 📋 Today's Delivery List · Senarai Penghantaran. A summary bar shows your plate and progress, like 3 / 8 delivered (and … problem if any failed), with a green progress bar.
  • Each customer is a card showing the customer name, the area, the number of invoice(s), and the invoice numbers.
  • If the lorry has the same crew assigned, your Driver (and Second Driver) boxes may fill in automatically. Check they are correct.
  • If nothing is found: you see No delivery order for this lorry today — it means the office has not issued the order yet. See Section 7.

5. Confirm each delivery (photo, then save) — do it at EVERY stop

Submit at each stop, as you arrive. The save time is recorded as your arrival time, so the office sees an accurate record. Do not wait and do them all at the end of the day.

For each customer card in the list:

  1. Get the customer to sign the invoice.
  2. On that customer's card, tap 📷 Take photo · Ambil foto. Your camera opens — take a clear photo of the signed invoice.
  3. The photo appears as a small thumbnail on the card, with 1 photo ready ✓ (it counts up if you add more).
  4. More than one invoice for this customer? Tap 📷 Add photo · Tambah and snap the next one (up to 10).
  5. Now tap the green ✓ Save — Delivered · Dihantar button.
  6. You will see Saving… · Menyimpan…. When done, the card turns green and shows ✓ Delivered, and the progress bar goes up.
  7. Drive to the next stop and repeat.

The green ✓ Save — Delivered button only appears after you have taken at least one photo. No photo = nothing to save. If you tap save with no photo you will be told Take a photo first · Sila ambil foto dulu.

6. Mark a delivery as a problem (failed)

If you cannot complete a delivery, use the problem button instead of the photo.

  1. On that customer's card, tap Problem.
  2. A row of reason buttons appears. Tap the one that fits.

The available reasons are exactly:

ButtonUse it when…
Shop closedThe shop is shut / no one there to receive.
RejectedThe customer refuses the goods.
PartialOnly part of the order could be delivered.
RescheduleDelivery is moved to another day.

After you tap a reason, the card is marked ✗ Problem (red). If saving fails you will see Could not save · Gagal simpan — try again.

7. What if there is no list?

Every delivery is issued by the office first. So if you pick your lorry and see 📋 No delivery order for this lorry today instead of a list, it means one of these:

  1. You picked the wrong lorry or the wrong date — check them at the top and fix.
  2. The office has not issued / imported today's order yet — call the office to release it, then re-pick your lorry to refresh.

There is no manual / walk-in submit. Every delivery must go through the office-issued list, so the supervisor dashboard stays accurate. If a customer is not on your list, contact the office to add it — do not try to submit it any other way.

8. How you know a delivery saved

You do not get a separate screen for each save — the confirmation is right on the list:

  • The customer's card turns green ✓ Delivered (or red ✗ Problem).
  • The progress bar moves up (for example 4 / 8 delivered).
  • That is your confirmation — drive to the next stop and repeat.

Quick check: the form sends in the background and cannot fully confirm by itself. To be safe, make sure your deliveries show up in the supervisor dashboard within a minute (or tell your supervisor). If a save looks stuck, re-pick your lorry to refresh the list.

If a save fails you may see Could not save · Gagal simpan — get a better signal and tap save again.

Tips

  • One stop, one submit. Photo → Save at every customer, before you drive off. Accurate arrival times protect you.
  • Always get the signature first, then photograph the invoice. An unsigned invoice is not valid.
  • Make the photo clear: good light, whole invoice in frame, signature readable.
  • Many invoices for one customer? Add them all to that customer (up to 10) before you tap save.
  • Check the date shows today before you start.
  • Stay online. You need a connection to load the staff list, load deliveries, and upload photos.
  • Watch the progress bar (e.g. 5 / 8 delivered) so you do not miss a stop.
  • After each save, glance that your delivery reached the supervisor dashboard within 1 minute.

Common situations

Two drivers sharing one lorry

  • The person doing the submitting picks themselves under Driver · Pemandu.
  • Put the other person under Second Driver · Pemandu Kedua.
  • Never put the same name in both — the form will block it (Driver and Second Driver cannot be the same person.).
  • If the lorry's crew was pre-assigned, both boxes may fill automatically — just confirm they are right.

A shop is closed

  • Do not skip it silently. On that customer's card tap Problem, then tap Shop closed.
  • The card turns red with ✗ Problem so the office knows why it was not delivered.
  • If it will be tried another day, use Reschedule instead.

Partial delivery

  • If only part of the order is delivered, tap Problem on that card, then tap Partial.
  • Take a photo of the signed invoice for what you did hand over, so there is a record.
  • Tell your supervisor what was short, so the rest can be arranged.
