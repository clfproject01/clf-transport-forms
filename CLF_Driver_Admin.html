New driver

Add driver writes the new joiner to the Drivers sheet and to Master_Control. No spreadsheet work is needed. Staff ID looks like TER 563 or KTN 701.

Resign removes the driver from the forms, the dashboard and the KPI at once. History is kept.

Rejoin brings a resigned driver back. Re-add shows instead when the driver has no staff ID row: it fills the name above, then enter the staff ID and branch and press Add driver.

No ID row: the driver is in Master_Control but missing from the Drivers sheet. Not in Master_Control: the forms will not show this driver.
